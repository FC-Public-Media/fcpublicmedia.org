# editing bay 2

A macOS bay, otherwise undescribed. It is empowered to schedule FCPM's Cablecast material, so part
of the Cablecast sync runs here; what runs, and how it starts, is not written down. Its `names` is
unfilled: on the machine, put `scutil --get LocalHostName` there, never the sticker. It claims
nothing else yet. Machines and profiles: [docs/station.md](../../docs/station.md).

The bays share a network drive of about 32 GB in some eight partitions of a little under 4 GB, one
per box. Where it mounts here, and which partition is this bay's, is not established.
