# The attic: decorum

`status: draft`. Narrows `../CONSTITUTION.md` for the attic (`fcpm library where attic`).

## Why things are here

The attic is what came down from FCPM's Google Drive and has not been placed. Two kinds of thing,
for two reasons:

- **The board's older records**, evicted from the Drive because they were last changed in 2025 or
  before. The Drive was too easy for members to reach, and the move is toward Microsoft 365.
- **Members' projects**, run by board members for the members, uploaded to the Drive to be staged.
  Uploads from our computers count against the organization's space wherever they go, so we take
  them off and stage them ourselves. The x-ray of their arrival is our record of what each was.

We are not sure how all of it got there.

## What it is for

Staging. Records wait here to be placed (SharePoint, the vault, or let go). Projects wait here until
they are handed back or moved on. Nothing here is the attic's to keep for good.

## Expect it to be sensitive

Assume a name or a number in anything. So:

- Read a file only to say what kind of thing it is: a board record (which kind), a member's project,
  media, an installer, unknown. Never what it says.
- A project is studied as a file format, as something someone did: its folders, its kinds of file,
  its timeline. Write that on the project's top folder.
- Anything that looks like account details, logins, or keys: note it as a candidate for sealing (the
  keychain wing), and nothing of its contents.
- Who expected to reach each thing is the open question here. Note the role you can infer, or ask.
