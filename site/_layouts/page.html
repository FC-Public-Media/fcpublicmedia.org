The title still exists in , which is what a tab, a bookmark, a search result and a shared link all read. What is gone is the on-page copy of it. Pages under this layout therefore have no <h1> and start at <h2>. That is deliberate, and tests/smoke.spec.js knows it — an accessibility audit will flag it, so it should be a decision on the record rather than a surprise.
