## 1

Above `<style>`

The idle screen. Open it, press F11 (or ⌃⌘F), leave it.

It is not a screensaver and deliberately not built as one. A .saver bundle
is macOS-only, a .scr is Windows-only, both need signing or a warning
dialog, and neither can be changed without walking to the machine. This is
one file that any browser on the panel will show full screen, which means
the thing on the wall is the thing in the repository.

It is also the shape the panel by the door eventually needs anyway — the
guest network, a check-in prompt, what is on air. Those are somebody's live
errand, so this ships as the brand and a marked slot rather than as a guess
at their content. See "THE SLOT" below.

No build step, no dependencies, no network. It has to come up on a machine
that has been off for a month and may not have joined the wifi yet.
