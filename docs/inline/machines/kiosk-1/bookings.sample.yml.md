bookings.sample.yml — MADE UP. Nobody booked any of this.

Stands in for the booking calendars until they are connected, so the
studio map on the welcome screen can be looked at and argued about. While
node.yml says `bookings: sample`, the screen shows it plainly, so nobody
standing in the lobby mistakes it for the real day: every pill it puts up
is for "Sample". Autumn, 2026-09-23: names on the upcoming slots are fine,
and "Sample" is a name.

DELIBERATELY SPARSE. People come in one at a time, and a full week made the
map look busier than the studio is. Autumn, 2026-09-23: "I want to see it
less full." A few bookings a week, never two at once, leaves some stations
with nothing booked, and that is how the "who can help, and when" line gets
seen.

The shape is the rota's: a weekly pattern in the studio's local time.
`station` is a name from node.yml's stations.
