## 1

Above `- name: Video Studio`

The spaces, and what is in them.

Copy and square footage from the board president, August 2026. His words are
kept close to verbatim — this is the one part of the site not written by us,
and it reads better for it.

`area` is separate from `summary` on purpose. The number is structured so it
can be shown, sorted or joined against an activity later; the prose is what a
member actually reads. Neither has to be parsed out of the other.

Membership is required to reserve any of these.

THE FLOOR PLAN USES THE BUILDING'S LABELS, NOT OURS. On
assets/img/floor-plan.png the rooms read CONTROL ROOM, STUDIO and OFFICE:

  CONTROL ROOM  318 sq ft  ->  Editing Bays
  STUDIO        635 sq ft  ->  Video Studio
  OFFICE        113 sq ft  ->  Podcast Studio

The square footage matches, so the plan is right — it is named for the
building rather than for what we do in there. Anybody reading the page and
the plan together will hit that mismatch, which is why `plan_label` is here
and why the page captions it rather than leaving someone to work it out.

## 3

Above `- name: Editing Bays`

TWO STATIONS, IN ONE ROOM, AND "EDITING BAY" MEANS BOTH.

The word is stratified and that is not a mistake to tidy away: the room is
the editing bay, and each of the two computer stations in it is also an
editing bay. Both are what people here say.

There were three. One machine was retired, and the third lingered on the Wix
site as a bookable service — harmless there, because picking a station was a
soft reservation and staff just sat you at whichever was free. It is a real
number here, so it is two.

The stations are meant to be interchangeable and are both dual-monitor. What
differs is superficial and account-shaped — whose OneDrive is signed in — and
there is a plan to put them on a small LAN so work can move between them when
something goes wrong mid-session.
