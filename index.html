No carousel — every item is visible at once and nothing moves on its own. The hero photo is a background *behind* a solid panel, not underneath the text. That is deliberate: the old site put type directly on a busy, colorful studio shot, which made both the photo and the words hard to look at. Here the photo can be as colorful as it likes and the words stay legible. Drop a file at assets/img/studio.jpg and it appears. Until then the hero is type only, which is a perfectly good state rather than a broken one. {%- endcomment -%}
