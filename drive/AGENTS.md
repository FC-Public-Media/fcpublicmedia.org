# The drive: decorum

`status: draft`. Narrows `../CONSTITUTION.md` for the organization's Google Drive (`fcpm library where
drive`).

## Why things are here

Two reasons. The drive is the cloud slice people drop files into to reach another of our computers,
the cloud version of a shared drive on the LAN. It also still holds board documents, which are being
moved off because members could reach them too easily. The move is toward Microsoft 365. Uploads from
any of our computers count against the organization's space, wherever they go.

## What it is for

Passing files between our computers, and staging members' projects until we take them off and stage
them ourselves (see the attic). It is not a place to keep anything for good.

## Expect it to be sensitive

- It is streamed: most files are placeholders, and reading one downloads it. Never read a file here
  without a contract that names it. A set of files is a job for a script, never for hands.
- A name beginning `REMOVED TO LAN` marks something already taken off. Note it once, at the top of that
  entry, and go no further.
- Anything that looks like account details, logins or keys: note it as a candidate for sealing, and
  nothing of what it holds.
- Who expected to reach each thing is the open question here, as it is in the attic. Note the role you
  can infer, or ask.

## Target layout

None. The drive is being emptied, not sorted.
